What the workbook shows

What it costs us, in money paid to vendors, to serve one client for one month of AI-visibility monitoring, for three offering sizes, and how that cost compares with what competitors charge. It is an internal cost model at public list prices. It is not a price list, and it leaves out engineering, hosting staff, support and margin.

The sheets, in reading order

Backup (hidden)Every input, price, source and the arithmetic. Unhide it to trace any number.

Where to look

  • Offering / tier assumptions: Client Tiers & Economics, rows 6–9 (prompts, cadence, runs, Oxylabs plan) and rows 12–21 (surfaces switched on); summarised in “Three client tiers” on the Investor Summary.
  • Total cost to serve a client (COGS): Investor Summary rows 6–13, or Client Tiers & Economics row 38. Row 14 gives cost per tracked answer.
  • Actions / Recommendations cost: the line inside the stack; the options and their drivers on Actions & Models.
  • Competitor context: Market Benchmark; the ratio in H20–H21 is headroom, not a price recommendation.

Actuals, estimates and scenarios

Every figure carries a grade in its “Basis / grade” or “Note” column:

  • ACTUAL — measured: the tokens of the 14 model calls behind one onboarding, and the retry and check rates from 12 stored collection runs.
  • OFFICIAL VENDOR PRICING — list prices read on the vendors’ own pages (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Oxylabs, cloro), verified 2026-10-08.
  • CALCULATED — arithmetic on the two above; this covers the whole monitoring stack for every tier.
  • ESTIMATED / ASSUMPTION — not yet measured: all Actions-layer volumes (the layer has not run), tokens per Claude answer, hosting at $0, the 12-month spread of the one-time prompt build, ten clients sharing one Oxylabs plan, and the 2.8% retry rate applied to surfaces not yet wired. Section 6 of the Tiers sheet shows how far the Standard tier moves if these are wrong.

Colour code: blue text = input, yellow fill = the levers worth changing, green = linked from another sheet, black = formula. Change a blue cell on the Tiers sheet and every page follows.

How to interpret the outputs

  • The headline costs are usage at list prices: no negotiated rates, no fixed costs, no margin. The Oxylabs plan minimum ($30 a month) is shared across clients and only matters while the company tracks fewer than 20,000 answers a month in total.
  • Cost scales with cadence × surfaces × prompts; our own AI (building the prompt set) and the deterministic analysis are a rounding error, and the Actions layer adds a tenth of the Small tier and a few percent of Enterprise.
  • Only the 30-prompt ChatGPT configuration has run in production; the other surfaces are priced from the vendors’ rate cards, and no vendor discloses which model version answers a signed-out prompt.
